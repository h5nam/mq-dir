⏵ The gap

Your IDE shows you code. Your agent writes code. What about everything else?

A modern coding session is half source, half artifacts. Generated images, recorded demos, PDFs from product, screenshots from QA, exported transcripts, downloaded models, Whisper outputs, design specs. None of it lives where your code does — and the tools you have for it weren't built for this volume.

⏵ Features

Native. Fast. Persistent.

Every detail tuned for parallel work. Nothing reinvented, nothing decorative.

01

Up to 4 panes, fully independent

The Q-Dir heritage in a 2×2 grid. Each pane has its own folder, its own sort, its own history. Drag-to-move becomes muscle memory.

02

Tabs in every pane

Browser-style tabs per pane, drag between panes, ⌘T new, ⌘W close. The tab order persists across launches, naturally.

03

Inline preview pane

Images with pinch-zoom, video and audio with native QuickLook controls, PDF — all inside the window. No floating panel. No context loss.

04

VS Code-style sidebar tree

Favorites, mounted volumes, and Finder tags in a collapsible left panel. One click to navigate any pane.

05

Keyboard-first, always

Every action has a shortcut. ⌘1–4 focus a pane, ⌘⌥1–4 switch layout, Tab cycles focus, Space QuickLooks. Nothing is mouse-only.

06

First-class accessibility

VoiceOver row read order, full keyboard navigation, WCAG AA contrast, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion respected from day one.

07

Quiet, functional motion

No bouncy springs, no shimmer, no decorative gradients. Transitions stay under 200ms ease-out. Reduce Motion makes them zero.

08

iCloud-aware

Placeholder rows for files not yet downloaded. Click once to bring them down — progress shows inline, no modal interruption.

09

Native to the bone

System materials, SF Symbols, traffic-light controls, NSWindow chrome. Built with SwiftUI where it earns it. Not Electron. Not ported.

⏵ In motion

Watch how four panes change everything.

Click through the steps, or scroll. Each step is a real interaction in the app.

STEP 01
Start in a single pane
Cold launch — your last folder, sort, and scroll position. Ready to work.
STEP 02
Split to two panes
⌥⌘2 splits horizontally. The right pane opens at the same folder. Drag the divider to resize.
STEP 03
Open all four
⌥⌘4 expands to a 2×2 grid. Each pane is fully independent — own folder, own sort, own tabs.
STEP 04
Drag between panes
Move, copy with ⌥, alias with ⌘. The drop indicator shows exactly where files land.
STEP 05
Preview without leaving
Inline preview pane — images with zoom, video with QuickLook controls. No floating windows.
